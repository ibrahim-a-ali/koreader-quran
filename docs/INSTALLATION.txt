Quran PDF Helper - 0.1.0 (experimental)

Goal: Preserve the Quran's original page layout while enabling the tafseer and
word lookup features of https://github.com/zeeyado/quran-ebook in KOReader.

The two downloads are quran_pdf_helper_v0.1.0.zip and Quran_Tafsir.pdf.

1. Install KOReader on your device.
2. Extract quran_pdf_helper_v0.1.0.zip inside KOReader's plugins folder, so the
   resulting folder is plugins/quran_pdf.koplugin/.
3. Copy Quran_Tafsir.pdf to your device and open it in KOReader.
4. Visit https://github.com/zeeyado/quran-ebook to download dictionaries,
   tafseers and related resources. Follow its dictionary installation instructions.

Dictionaries are not bundled here. Long-press an ayah number or word to use the
corresponding lookup after installing the relevant dictionaries.

The Quran PDF was obtained from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex.
Its original page layout and vector artwork have been preserved. AI-assisted
processing enabled Arabic word selection/copying and added the information
required for ayah lookups.

The PDF and plugin have been tested in KOReader at different, randomly selected
locations. Not every word or every ayah has been tested, and the functionality
has not been completely verified. This remains an experimental release.

The plugin is adapted from Quran Helper v1.10 by zeeyado/quran-ebook.
Original credits and GPL-3.0 licence notices are retained.
